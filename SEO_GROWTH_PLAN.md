# SEO Growth Plan for CalEye

## Primary target
- Main keywords: Calorie Counter app, AI calorie tracker, Nutrition tracker
- Best ranking page: https://caleye.co/
- Supporting terms: AI calorie counter, food calorie scanner, photo calorie counter, calorie tracking app, macro tracker, nutrition tracker app

  - How to count calories from a food photo

- Add app-specific support pages:
  - CalEye for weight loss
  - CalEye for meal prep

  - CalEye AI calorie tracker for Android

## App store SEO
- Use "Calorie Counter app", "AI calorie tracker", and "Nutrition tracker" in the Google Play short description.
- Add screenshots with visible captions such as "Scan food", "Count calories", and "Track macros".
- Ask real users for reviews that naturally mention calories, food scanner, macros, and weight loss.

## Authority building
- Submit CalEye to startup directories, health app lists, Android app directories, and AI tool directories.
- Build links from fitness blogs, nutrition creators, university health projects, and app review sites.
- Publish original content using anonymized insights, such as common calorie tracking mistakes or food logging trends.

## Technical SEO checks
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Inspect the homepage URL after deployment and request indexing.
- Track impressions for "Calorie Counter app", "AI calorie tracker", and "Nutrition tracker", then expand pages around queries that start getting impressions.
- Keep page titles under roughly 60 characters and descriptions under roughly 160 characters when adding new pages.
